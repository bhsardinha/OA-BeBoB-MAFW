OpenAudio driver for the M-Audio FireWire 1814

A native driver stack for macOS 10.13.6 High Sierra. This installs:

  • the audio driver (/Library/Extensions/OpenAudioFW1814.kext)
  • a firmware-start and settings helper (a LaunchDaemon)
  • the OpenAudio FW1814 control panel (/Applications)
  • the M-Audio CoreMIDI driver and the device icons

Note: this is an experimental, unsigned driver. It only loads on a Mac with System Integrity Protection disabled (Recovery mode, csrutil disable). Quit audio and MIDI applications before installing. The M-Audio/Avid FireWire driver must not be installed at the same time.
