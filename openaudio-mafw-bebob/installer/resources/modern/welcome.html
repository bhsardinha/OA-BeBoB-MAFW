OpenAudio BeBoB MAFW @VERSION@ — Modern edition

A native driver stack for the M-Audio FireWire 1814 and FireWire Audiophile on macOS 11 to 15, Intel Macs. This installs:

  • the audio driver @DRIVER@ (/Library/Extensions/OpenAudioBeBoBMAFW.kext)
  • a firmware-start and settings helper (a LaunchDaemon)
  • the OA BeBoB MAFW control panel @PANEL@ in /Applications, with a read-only firmware check
  • the M-Audio CoreMIDI driver and the device icons

Requirements

macOS11 Big Sur to 15 Sequoia, on Intel Macs (tested on 14.8.9 Sonoma). Not for Apple Silicon Macs or macOS 26. For macOS 10.13, use the Classic edition.
MacAn Intel Mac with a FireWire port, or with Thunderbolt and Apple's Thunderbolt to FireWire Adapter. Tested: MacBook Air (Early 2015, Thunderbolt 2 with the adapter).
InterfacesM-Audio FireWire Audiophile (tested) and FireWire 1814 (same driver as the Classic edition, not yet tested on macOS 11 or later). Other M-Audio FireWire models are not supported.
SecuritySystem Integrity Protection must allow unsigned kernel extensions (Recovery mode: csrutil disable, or csrutil enable --without kext), and the driver must be allowed in Privacy & Security after installing.

Any earlier version is replaced: there is no need to uninstall it first, and your settings are kept. Nothing is unloaded while it runs.

After installing, macOS asks you to allow the driver: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, click Allow next to the OpenAudio software, then restart. A new driver version needs this again.

Note: this is an experimental, unsigned driver. Quit audio and MIDI applications before installing. The M-Audio/Avid FireWire driver must not be installed at the same time.
