Installed: OpenAudio BeBoB MAFW @VERSION@ — Modern edition

1. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Allow next to the OpenAudio software.
2. Restart the Mac, then power on the interface.

Open OA BeBoB MAFW from Applications for the control panel; the interface appears in Audio MIDI Setup.

Driver @DRIVER@, panel @PANEL@.

To remove everything run sudo /Library/Application\ Support/OpenAudio/uninstall.sh, then restart.
