OpenAudio BeBoB MAFW @VERSION@ — Classic edition

A native driver stack for the M-Audio FireWire 1814, FireWire Audiophile and FireWire 410 on macOS 10.13.6 High Sierra. This installs:

  • the audio driver @DRIVER@ (/Library/Extensions/OpenAudioBeBoBMAFW.kext)
  • a firmware-start and settings helper (a LaunchDaemon)
  • the OA BeBoB MAFW control panel @PANEL@ in /Applications, with a read-only firmware check
  • the M-Audio CoreMIDI driver and the device icons

Requirements

macOS10.13 High Sierra (tested on 10.13.6). 10.14 Mojave and 10.15 Catalina are allowed but not tested. For macOS 11 or later, use the Modern edition.
MacAn Intel Mac with a FireWire port, or with Thunderbolt and Apple's Thunderbolt to FireWire Adapter. Tested: iMac (Mid 2010, FireWire 800) and MacBook Air (Mid 2012, with the adapter).
InterfacesM-Audio FireWire 1814 and FireWire Audiophile, both tested, alone and together. FireWire 410: new in 0.4.0, tested alone on macOS 10.13.6 (MIDI, S/PDIF and sleep not yet). Other M-Audio FireWire models are not supported.
SecuritySystem Integrity Protection disabled (Recovery mode: csrutil disable).

Any earlier version (OpenAudio FW1814 0.1.0, OpenAudio BeBoB MAFW 0.2.x / 0.3.x) is replaced: there is no need to uninstall it first, and your settings are kept. Nothing is unloaded while it runs: the Mac must be restarted at the end.

Note: this is an experimental, unsigned driver. Quit audio and MIDI applications before installing. The M-Audio/Avid FireWire driver must not be installed at the same time.
