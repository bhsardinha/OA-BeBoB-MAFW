OpenAudio BeBoB MAFW @VERSION@

A native driver stack for the M-Audio FireWire 1814 and FireWire Audiophile on macOS 10.13.6 High Sierra. This installs:

  • the audio driver @DRIVER@ (/Library/Extensions/OpenAudioBeBoBMAFW.kext)
  • a firmware-start and settings helper (a LaunchDaemon)
  • the OA BeBoB MAFW control panel @PANEL@ in /Applications, with a read-only firmware check
  • the M-Audio CoreMIDI driver and the device icons

Any earlier version (OpenAudio FW1814 0.1.0, OpenAudio BeBoB MAFW 0.2.x) is replaced: there is no need to uninstall it first, and your settings are kept. Nothing is unloaded while it runs: the Mac must be restarted at the end.

Note: this is an experimental, unsigned driver. It only loads on a Mac with System Integrity Protection disabled (Recovery mode, csrutil disable). Quit audio and MIDI applications before installing. The M-Audio/Avid FireWire driver must not be installed at the same time.
