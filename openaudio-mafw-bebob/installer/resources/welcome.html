OpenAudio BeBoB MAFW @VERSION@ — Classic edition

A native driver stack for the M-Audio FireWire 1814, FireWire Audiophile and FireWire 410 on macOS 10.13.6 High Sierra. This installs:

  • the audio driver @DRIVER@ (/Library/Extensions/OpenAudioBeBoBMAFW.kext)
  • a firmware-start and settings helper (a LaunchDaemon)
  • the OA BeBoB MAFW control panel @PANEL@ in /Applications, with a read-only firmware check and, for the FireWire 1814, the Advanced window
  • the M-Audio CoreMIDI driver and the device icons

Requirements

macOS10.13 High Sierra to 10.15 Catalina. For macOS 11 or later, use the Modern edition.
MacAn Intel Mac with a FireWire port, or with Thunderbolt and Apple's Thunderbolt to FireWire Adapter.
InterfacesM-Audio FireWire 1814, FireWire Audiophile and FireWire 410, alone or together. Other M-Audio FireWire models are not supported.
SecuritySystem Integrity Protection disabled (Recovery mode: csrutil disable).

Any earlier version (OpenAudio FW1814 0.1.0, OpenAudio BeBoB MAFW 0.2.x to 0.5.0) is replaced: there is no need to uninstall it first, and your settings are kept. Nothing is unloaded while it runs: the Mac must be restarted at the end.

Note: this is an experimental, unsigned driver. Quit audio and MIDI applications before installing. The M-Audio/Avid FireWire driver must not be installed at the same time.
