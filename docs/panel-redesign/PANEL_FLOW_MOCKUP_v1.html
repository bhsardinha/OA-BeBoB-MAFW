Flow: a proposal for the OA BeBoB MAFW panel (mockup v1)

Interactive sketch of the organisation in docs/PANEL_REDESIGN_PLAN_v1.md. Same palette and type as the current panel; meters are simulated and nothing talks to a driver.
Click a mix tile (or press 1–6) to work on that mix · drag faders, pans and the knob · ⌥-click or double-click a fader for 0 dB · click a strip name to rename or hide it · ⋯ opens an output's rare controls · click the Phones A/B row to press the front button · try 192 kHz on the FW1814 from the clock pill.
